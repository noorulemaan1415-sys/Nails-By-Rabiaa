Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions about extensions, durability, bridal sets, or aftercare? Here are quick answers.

How long do acrylic extensions and 3D nail art last? ▼
Our acrylic extensions and 3D bow/pearl charms typically last 3 to 5 weeks without lifting or chipping. We use industrial-grade salon bonding resins to ensure charms don't snag on luxury chiffon or lehengas.
How can I book an appointment with Rabiaa? ▼
You can book directly via WhatsApp at +92 333 0213647 or by filling out the booking form on this website. Because we dedicate private 1-on-1 time to each client, we recommend booking 2-4 days in advance.
What is the difference between BIAB and Acrylics? ▼
BIAB (Builder in a Bottle) is a thick strengthening gel applied over your natural nails to reinforce them and help them grow without adding plastic tips. Acrylics are sculpted extensions that add custom length and shape (such as long Coffin or Stiletto).
Do you coordinate bridal nails with wedding mehndi/henna? ▼
Yes! Our signature bridal sets (as featured in our client reviews) are specifically designed to complement intricate mehndi designs and bridal jewelry with custom French tips, micro-pearls, and 3D bows.
What hygiene protocols do you follow? ▼
All metal tools and e-file drill bits undergo clinical autoclave sterilization in sealed medical pouches. Buffers and nail files are strictly single-use for each client.
